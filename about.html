A familiar, layer-based SVG editor that runs in your browser

Layers, effects, blend modes, gradients, patterns, rich type, symbols and clipping masks, with clean, standard SVG out. Nothing to install and no account: your drawings stay in your browser.

Open the editor See what it does

Why SVGCraft

Most SVG editors are either desktop suites or diagram tools. SVGCraft is a lightweight editor for illustrations, icons and UI art, with the controls and shortcuts of the tools designers already know.

Familiar

The tools, menus, panels and shortcuts you already know: Free Transform, effects, blend modes, Pass Through groups and live corners.

Real SVG

Everything you make is plain SVG that other editors and browsers read. SVGCraft's own settings travel inside the file, so it opens again fully editable.

Private

It's four static files with no server. Open drawings and libraries are kept in your browser, not uploaded anywhere.

Light or dark

Follows your system theme, or pick Dark or Light from View → Theme. On a phone it becomes a viewer: play the animations, show and hide objects, try it on white or black, read and copy its SVG source, see its metadata, and share it.

Features

The highlights, with a screenshot of each. The full list follows.

Draw and edit paths

  • Pen for corners and smooth curves, Pencil for freehand lines with smoothing.
  • Shape tools: rectangles with live rounded corners, ellipses, triangles, polygons and stars, lines with arrowheads, and custom shapes from the Symbols library, dragged or typed to an exact size.
  • Direct Selection on any path or shape: drag points and handles, add, delete, switch corner and smooth; pick points one at a time with the arrow, or several with a rectangle, an ellipse or a lasso from its toolbar flyout, Shift to add and Alt to take away.
  • Eraser that deletes objects or cuts stroked lines.
  • Combine Shapes: Unite, Subtract Front, Intersect and Exclude. The Shape Builder merges or deletes the pieces overlapping shapes make, by dragging across them.

Colour, gradients and patterns

  • Every shape's own fill and stroke, each with its colour and alpha, picked from the drawing tools' options bar, or edited in Properties.
  • Linear and radial gradients with a stop editor and handles on the canvas.
  • Pattern fills, and Define Pattern to turn any selection into a tile.
  • Stretch a shape and its gradient keeps its angle while its pattern tiles keep their size. Pick presets, patterns and custom shapes from picture tiles.
  • Swatches, Gradients and Patterns libraries kept in your browser. Export them in one zip with GIMP palette, CSS and SVG files for other apps, and import palettes and gradients from them. Duplicates are skipped, and name clashes ask before replacing anything.
  • Global swatches: edit the colour once and every object painted with it follows.
  • Eyedropper that copies a style or samples the exact on-screen colour.

Layers and effects

  • A Layers panel with groups as folders, drag to reorder, rename, show and hide. Alt-click an eye to show just that object; drag rows onto New to copy them or onto the bin to delete them.
  • Effects: drop shadow, inner shadow, outer and inner glow, colour overlay and Gaussian blur, with a live preview. Double-click a row to open it, pick one from the Effect menu, and copy and paste effects from object to object.
  • All 16 standard blend modes, plus Pass Through for groups.
  • Lock position or lock everything, per object or per group.
  • An Object menu with Object via Copy and Object via Cut: copy or cut whole objects, a shape out of its group, or the segments between points you pick.
  • Merge Down and Merge Visible, staying vector: alike shapes become one path, the rest one group.
  • Group by Colour sorts a drawing into one group per colour, keeping how it looks.
  • Clipping masks: clip objects to the shape or text on top of them.

Type and symbols

  • Point and paragraph text, edited right on the canvas, with Character and Paragraph panels.
  • Per-character styling, the whole Google Fonts catalogue (about 1,900 families) and your installed fonts.
  • Text on a path and Create Outlines to turn text into shapes.
  • Symbols: reusable artwork with instances that update together.
  • A Symbols library of 80 built-in icons, plus your own, exported as an SVG sprite for the web.

Clean SVG in and out

  • SVG Source as a split view beside the canvas: syntax-coloured (CSS too), highlighting the selected object's markup, with find, copy and download. Edit it and apply it to the drawing, or start one with File → New from Text… by pasting SVG code.
  • Open, import, drag and drop or paste SVG and images. Imports are sanitised.
  • Image Trace turns a bitmap into shapes, one per colour, stacked so there are no gaps.
  • Export SVG, a clean minified SVG for the web, or PNG at 1×, 2× and 4×.
  • File Info for title, author, copyright and other metadata, stored the standard way.
  • Effects, locks, blend modes and dashes all survive a save and reopen.

Animation

  • Animate any object or group from the Animation tab, with presets to pick from: fade, slide, pop and zoom in or out, spin, pulse, bounce, shake, float and blink, draw a line on or off, march its dashes, or cycle its colour.
  • Set the length, delay, how many times or forever, direction, back and forth, and easing. Objects can have several animations at once.
  • Chain one to start after the one above, and drag to reorder them.
  • Preview it on the canvas with ▶ Play.
  • Saved as standard SVG animation that plays in every current browser, with no script, and reopens editable.
  • Opens other files' animations too: a spin, a fade or a colour change becomes the matching preset, ready to edit.
  • Move along path: send an object along a line you draw, turning to face the way it goes if you like.
  • Stagger a group's objects or a text's letters one after another, or type text out letter by letter with Typewriter.
  • Drag an animation's pivot to turn or grow it about any point.
  • Morph one shape into another, and effects like Wipe in, Glow pulse, Shine, Ripple, Gradient flow, Pattern scroll and Flipbook.
  • Start an animation on a click or hover, after another object's, or with a pause between its runs.

A comfortable workspace

  • Document tabs, each with its own undo history and view, restored when you come back.
  • A * on a tab when the drawing has unsaved changes.
  • Open Recent: the last ten files you opened or saved.
  • New Document with presets for web, social, mobile and print, any units, orientation and a background colour.
  • Zoom tool, rulers, guides, grid and snap, and a pixel grid with snap to pixel.
  • Icon tools: New Icon templates for Material, Android, iOS, macOS and favicons, margins as guides, keylines, and a live preview at 16 to 128 px on light, dark or transparent backgrounds, masked the way iOS and Android crop app icons, and app icon export for the web, iOS, Android and macOS in one zip.
  • Panels with icon tabs: fold any group to its tabs, or collapse the whole column to a strip of icons that pop each panel out, and drag the dividers between groups to resize them. A Window menu lists every panel, and Reset Workspace puts it all back.
  • Menu items and buttons grey out until they can run on what's selected, and their shortcuts with them.
  • Right-click menus on the canvas and in the Layers panel: what the selection can do, and a list of the objects under the pointer to pick a covered one.
  • Every dialog drags by its title bar. Close the last tab for a blank workspace.
  • Tools in the usual toolbar order, with pointers to match: the Selection arrow, the white Direct Selection arrow, the marquee cross and lasso with + and −, the pen's * and o.
  • Help built in: Help → SVGCraft Help (F1), searchable, and a ? on every dialog and panel that opens its topic.
  • Tooltips on every tool, and dark and light themes.

Everything else

Transform and arrange

  • Free Transform with exact position, size, angle and skew.
  • Transform → Scale, Rotate, Skew, Flip, Rotate 90° / 180°.
  • Transform Again, and Again with Copy for repeats.
  • Align and distribute, to the selection or the artboard.
  • Group, ungroup, z-order, duplicate, nudge.

Strokes

  • Width, caps and joins, right under the stroke colour in Properties → Appearance.
  • Align a closed shape's stroke inside, centre or outside.
  • Solid, dashed, dotted, long dash or custom dash patterns.

Selecting and cropping

  • Select All, Deselect and Inverse.
  • Marquee selection; Ctrl/⌘-click picks a shape inside a group, and double-clicking a group isolates it to work inside.
  • Fit the artboard to the selection or the drawing, counting strokes and effects.
  • Edit Colours: Desaturate, Black & White…, Threshold… and Convert to Grayscale, changing the colours in the file itself, images included.
  • Scale Document scales the whole drawing; Document Setup resizes the artboard around an anchor.
  • Work in pixels, millimetres, centimetres, inches or points, with page presets (A4, US Letter…) saved at their real size for print and cutting.

Safe to experiment

  • Undo and redo for every change, and a History panel naming each step: click one to go back to it.
  • Drawings saved in your browser as you work.
  • Works offline once you've opened it, and installs as an app on your computer or phone.
  • Tells you when there's a new version, and updates with one click.
  • Unsaved changes are flagged before a tab closes.

Keyboard shortcuts

On a Mac, use ⌘ where it says Ctrl. Browsers keep a few keys for themselves (such as Ctrl+T), so a few of the usual shortcuts use Alt instead.
